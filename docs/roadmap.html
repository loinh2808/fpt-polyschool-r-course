Learning Roadmap

Recommended Pathway for R for Research and Data Analytics

Learning Roadmap

This roadmap presents the recommended learning sequence for the R for Research and Data Analytics course. Learners may complete the modules sequentially or select modules according to their research needs.

NoteHow to Use This Roadmap

Start with the foundation modules if you are new to R or research data analysis. Move to the advanced modules when you already understand data cleaning, descriptive statistics, regression, visualization, and basic research reporting.

4. Suggested Pathways by Learner Type

Lecturers and Instructors

Recommended sequence:

  1. Module 1: Introduction to R and Data Management
  2. Module 2: Data Cleaning and Preparation
  3. Module 3: Descriptive Statistics and Group Comparisons
  4. Module 6: Data Visualization and Reporting
  5. A15: Reproducible Research
TipBest For Teaching

This pathway is suitable for lecturers who want to use R for classroom teaching, student projects, and research supervision.

Graduate Students and PhD Candidates

Recommended sequence:

  1. Module 1 to Module 6
  2. A2: Advanced Regression
  3. A4: Advanced SEM
  4. A11: Meta-analysis
  5. A12: Bibliometric Analysis
  6. A15: Reproducible Research
TipBest For Thesis and Publication

This pathway supports thesis writing, manuscript preparation, and publication-oriented research.

Applied Researchers

Recommended sequence:

  1. Module 1 to Module 6
  2. A1: Descriptive Diagnostics
  3. A2: Advanced Regression
  4. A6: Monte Carlo Simulation
  5. A13: Experimental Design and ANOVA
  6. A14: Mixed Models
TipBest For Research Projects

This pathway is useful for research teams working with field data, survey data, experimental data, or longitudinal datasets.

Data Analysts

Recommended sequence:

  1. Module 1 to Module 4
  2. Module 6
  3. A2: Advanced Regression
  4. A3: Ridge, LASSO, and Elastic Net
  5. A9: Visualization Gallery
  6. A15: Reproducible Research
TipBest For Analytics Practice

This pathway focuses on data preparation, modelling, prediction, visualization, and reproducible reporting.

6. Final Capstone

At the end of the course, learners should complete a final research analytics report that includes:

  • Dataset overview
  • Data cleaning summary
  • Descriptive statistics
  • Group comparison
  • Regression model
  • PLS-SEM or advanced model
  • Publication-ready figures
  • Academic-style results section
  • Reproducible Quarto report

Final Output

The recommended final product is:

Final_Research_Analytics_Report.html

This report should demonstrate that the learner can move from raw data to research interpretation and academic reporting.
