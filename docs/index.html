R for Research and Data Analytics

Project-Based Learning for Research, Statistics, Data Analytics, Visualization and Academic Reporting

# Research Workflow

```{mermaid}
flowchart TD

A[Import Data]
--> B[Clean Data]

B --> C[Describe Data]

C --> D[Visualize Patterns]

D --> E[Model Relationships]

E --> F[Test Theoretical Models]

F --> G[Advanced Analytics]

G --> H[Write Results]

H --> I[Reproducible Report]
```
